# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-24
- First ship, from the Go deeper branch (`deeper/chapters`, which includes the first version below).
- Go deeper chapters under every stop: 457 chapters, about 15 hours, each on one of twelve topics.
- Recorded narration in the Kokoro af_heart voice for all 623 tracks (overviews, stops and chapters), uploaded to R2 separately.

## 0.1.0 — not shipped on its own
- First version, started from Walk NYC's code (walk-nyc `main` at 772269a plus the Go deeper app support from its `deeper/extended-layer` branch).
- 22 Chicago tours, 144 stops, about 6 hours of narration; every stop's position checked against OpenStreetMap.
- Chicago look: tour badges in CTA 'L' line colors, a Chicago flag light blue accent, and a new icon.
- Chicago pronunciation fixes for the phone voice and the recorded voice.
